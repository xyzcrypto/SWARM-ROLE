# Audit 2026-10-07: security, bugs, speed, SEO

A read-only audit of the whole codebase (all 117 API routes and 30 pages), plus mobile
speed measured in a real browser at 4x CPU throttle on a local production build with no
database attached. Every finding below was checked against the code; file:line references
are as of `main` at 559cc8f.

**Ratings:** Security **7/10** · Bugs and code health **7/10** · Speed **6.5/10** · SEO
**5.5/10** · **Overall 6.5/10**. Fixing the P0 and P1 lists below should put it around 8.5.

Status key: ✅ fixed (with PR) · ⬜ open.

## P2: when there is time

- **Security**
  - **S4:** push-subscribe accepts any endpoint, allowing blind SSRF through follow notifications. Allowlist the push-service hosts.
  - **S5:** anyone can lock any account with 5 bad logins (known F-010).
  - **S6:** a blocked user can still edit sent DMs.
  - **S8:** chat translate has no daily AI cap.
  - **S9:** the cron secret is accepted in the URL query.
  - **S10:** avatar and image hosts allow any `*.supabase.co`.
  - **S11:** password-reset timing reveals whether an account exists.
  - **S12:** account delete needs no password.
  - **S13:** `users:roles` can remove permissions the actor doesn't hold.
  - **Minor:** OTP reuse race, CSV escaping, `unsafe-inline` CSP (known F-015), staff-only tiptap/prosemirror advisories.
- **Speed**
  - The AI button animates `box-shadow` forever (paint every frame).
  - The chat list re-renders every row on typing and presence changes.
  - The Lenis rAF loop never sleeps.
  - Lazy chunks (command palette, customize sheet, anime.js) are fetched on every page.
  - `bundle-report.mjs` misses about 212 KB of root chunks.
  - `AudioContext` unlocks even with sound off.
  - Missing indexes: `Comment(userId, createdAt)`, `Post(authorId)`.
- **Bugs**
  - B6: light-theme `text-white` icons on dark overlays.
  - B7: latent #418 in `/tools` calculators and Journal Risk.
  - B8: share hrefs built from `window` during render.
  - B9: chat edited-tooltip timezone; side effects inside a `setMessages` updater.
  - B10: Featured cards are not keyboard-operable.
  - B11: `/` and `/research` read the DB at build.
  - About 30 em dashes in site copy.
  - `chat-time-format.test.ts` times out in full runs.
  - Open decisions: the RiseX card says 10% while its calculator uses 20–30%; Arcus, Sodex and RiseX show a "sellable at TGE" line with no unlock data.
- **SEO**
  - SEO7: thin article JSON-LD (image, author url, paywall markup).
  - SEO8: unknown protocol slugs return 200.
  - SEO9: about 5,000 empty profiles in the sitemap.
  - SEO10: `lastModified` is always "now".
  - SEO11: `/saved` and `/portfolio` are indexable.
  - SEO12: a second `<h1>` from markdown and the news modal.
  - SEO13: tags aren't links.

## Biggest SEO opportunity

Real per-programme pages at `/points/[venue]`, built from `DEX_REGISTRY`, each with the
calculator pre-selected, a server-rendered explainer, an FAQ and JSON-LD. They target
low-competition searches like "Variational airdrop calculator" and "RiseX points value".
After that: server-render the coin and protocol pages, registry-driven comparison pages,
research tag hubs, and per-tool landing pages.

## Checked and clean

- **API routes:** every mutating route has its rate limit, zod and auth gates; no IDOR found.
- **Content and accounts:** premium bodies never leak; login/OTP lockout and timing defences
  hold; uploads are checked by bytes, never SVG.
- **XSS and SSRF:** guarded via `safeHref`, JSON-LD escaping and `safe-fetch`.
- **Headers:** HSTS, `frame-ancestors 'none'` and `nosniff` are set.
- **Cache-Control:** never public on personalised data.
- **Code:** points and calculator maths are correct; no N+1 queries; cache windows match the
  `cost-check` skill.
- **Checks:** typecheck is clean; 1492/1494 tests pass (the 2 failures are a flaky timeout).
