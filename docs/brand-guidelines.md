## Voice & tone
- Premium, confident, enterprise-grade, human. Crypto-native but trustworthy.
- **No AI-generated tells**: no em dashes (`—`) in body copy — use commas,
  periods or colons. Avoid generic filler.
- Keep the single intentional Sparkles in the hero eyebrow (owner-approved).

## Visual identity
- **Black + Gold** is the core identity (premium, expensive feel).
- Gold = brand accent (wordmark, headings, eyebrows, badges, premium/auth buttons).
- **Premium green** = primary action CTAs (conversion). Green/red = market data only.
- Deep black canvas, soft-white type, restrained glow, premium shadows, glass.
